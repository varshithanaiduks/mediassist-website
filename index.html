TRANSPARENCY FIRST

Privacy & safety

MediAssist is a demonstration prototype. It is not a hospital, clinic, emergency service or medical device. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or replace a qualified healthcare professional.

Your information

In this prototype, symptom answers are processed in your browser and are not sent to a server or saved to an account. Avoid entering identifying or highly sensitive information. The external map search opens Google Maps and is governed by Google's own privacy practices.

When to seek help

If you have severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, seek urgent medical care. In India, call 112 for emergency assistance. Do not delay care to use this website.
